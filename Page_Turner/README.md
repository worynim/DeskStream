# 📖 DeskStream Page Turner (BLE Page Turner) - v1.1.0

A Bluetooth remote: hold the DeskStream board upright and press the buttons with your thumb to **turn pages of web novels and webtoons or flip through YouTube Shorts**.
It also works as a smartphone camera shutter (including a delayed shutter) and a PC presentation clicker. No app needed — just pair it over Bluetooth.

## ✨ Key Features

- **7 per-app modes**: YouTube · Kakao Webtoon/Novel · Series Webtoon/Novel · Camera · PPT (verified on Android)
- **3 connected-device slots**: remembers a phone, tablet and PC; switch with two button presses
- **Settings with buttons only**: wheel steps, arrow key count, gap, delayed shutter time, button beep — kept after power-off
- Outline-only screens that don't dazzle in the dark, screen off after 1 minute · deep sleep after 5 minutes

## 🎮 Buttons

Holding the device upright, BTN1 to BTN4 run from top to bottom.

| Button | Short | Long |
|---|---|---|
| **BTN1** | Previous page / scroll up / shutter | — |
| **BTN2** | Next page / scroll down / delayed shutter | — |
| **BTN3** | Next mode | Choose device (slot) |
| **BTN4** | Settings menu | — |

Wake it from deep sleep with **BTN1 or BTN2**.

## 🚀 Setup

1. Pick it in the [web flasher](https://worynim.github.io/DeskStream/), or build it yourself.
2. Turn on the board and pair **`DS Page Turner`** in your phone's Bluetooth settings.
3. Choose the mode for your app with BTN3. For Kakao and Series novels, turn on **page turning with volume keys** in the app.
